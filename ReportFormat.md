## Report Structure
The submitted report must follow a formal academic structure.
1. Cover page
2. Abstract
3. Table of contents
4. Table of figures
5. Table of abbreviations
6. Introduction
7. Software details
8. Design and implementation
9. Secure development and penetration testing
10. Proof of concept
11. Conclusion
12. References


##  References
A minimum of fifteen academic or professional references must be included.
1. Books
2. Research papers
3. Security standards
4. OWASP documentation
5. Vendor and professional security resources
Proper in-text citations are mandatory using CU Harvard or CU APA style.
8. Evidence Quality Requirements
All submitted evidence must meet quality standards.
1. Screenshots must be clear and readable
2. Evidence must demonstrate deterministic and repeatable behaviour
9. Enforcement Notice
Failure to comply with requirements related to secure development, internal
penetration testing, evidence quality, documentation standards, or academic
integrity may result in penalties.
1. Mark deductions
2. Section invalidation
3. Rejection of the entire submission in severe cases
